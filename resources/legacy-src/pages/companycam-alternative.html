CRM, Jobs & Revenue Workflow

Knockio: More Than a CompanyCam Competitor

CompanyCam is built around photo documentation, project progress, team updates, AI-generated notes and reports, payments, and especially strong image annotation tools with arrows, circles, text, and measurements. Knockio, by contrast, is publicly positioned as a door-to-door sales software and field service CRM with lead tracking, territories, proposals, scheduling, dispatch, work orders, and invoicing. That makes CompanyCam the stronger image documentation tool, but Knockio the much broader operating system.
