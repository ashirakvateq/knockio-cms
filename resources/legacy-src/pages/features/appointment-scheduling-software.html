Appointment Scheduling Software

Double bookings, no shows, and a schedule that lives in someone's head instead of an actual system. Sound familiar? Connect your existing calendar or use Knockio's built in calendar to book appointments, see who is available in real time, assign jobs to the right person, and send automatic reminders, so your schedule basically runs itself.

Does appointment scheduling software for contractors need its own calendar?
Not necessarily. Knockio's appointment scheduling software can connect to the calendar your team already uses, or you can use Knockio's built in calendar if you do not have one yet. Either way, you get real time availability views, conflict free assignment based on skill or territory, automated reminders for customers and crews, and support for recurring appointments, all tied directly to the job record.

Yes. Knockio can connect to your existing calendar, or you can use Knockio's built in calendar to manage appointments.
Can I see who is available before booking an appointment?
Yes. Knockio shows real time availability for your team, helping you assign appointments to the right person without conflicts.
Does Knockio send appointment reminders?
Yes. Knockio can automatically send reminders to customers and team members ahead of scheduled appointments to reduce no shows.
Can I set up recurring appointments?
Yes. Recurring appointments are supported, which is great for maintenance plans like pool care, lawn care, and pest control treatments.
