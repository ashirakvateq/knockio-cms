What kinds of reports can home service businesses build with Knockio?
Knockio lets home service businesses build custom reports based on their own rules, covering things like conversion rates, rep performance by territory, time spent in the field, leads that passed through specific statuses, forecasted revenue, incoming and outgoing payments, and employee check in and check out times. Because the reports are built on custom rules rather than fixed templates, you can create the exact report your business needs instead of settling for whatever a platform like ServiceTitan or Jobber decided to pre build.

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can I build a custom report instead of using a preset template?
Yes. Knockio lets you create custom rules and conditions to generate a report, so you are not limited to a fixed set of preset templates.
Can I build a report on lead conversion rates?
Yes. You can create a conversion report broken down by rep, territory, source, or any other rule that matters to your business.
Can I see how much time each rep spends in the field?
Yes. Knockio can generate a report showing how much time each rep actually spends in the field, based on real activity and location data.
Can I build a report showing leads that went through a specific status?
Yes. You can create a report that shows every lead or job that passed through a specific status, which helps identify exactly where deals are stalling.
Does Knockio support revenue forecasting?
Yes. You can build forecasted revenue reports based on your pipeline data, giving you a realistic view of expected revenue going forward.
Can I track payments through reports?
Yes. Knockio supports reports covering incoming, outgoing, due, and paid payments, giving you a clear and current financial picture at all times.
Can I track employee check in and check out times?
Yes. You can build a report on employee check in and check out activity, useful for payroll, accountability, and scheduling decisions.
