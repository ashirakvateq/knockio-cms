Yes. Knockio includes built in payment processing that lets home service businesses accept tap to pay straight from a technician's phone, send text to pay links for customers who are not on site, and process credit card and ACH payments. Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Does Knockio have its own payment processor?
Yes. Knockio includes its own built-in payment processing, supporting credit cards, ACH, tap to pay, and text to pay, with no need to connect a separate third party processor.
Can I record payments that were collected outside the app, like cash or check?
Yes. You can log manual payments against any invoice for accurate accounting and job financial tracking, even when the payment did not go through Knockio.
Can I send a customer a link to pay remotely?
Yes. Text to pay and online payment links let customers pay securely from their phone or computer, even if they are not on site when the job wraps up.
How does Knockio help me get paid faster?
By combining instant invoicing, multiple payment options, and automated reminders in one platform, Knockio removes the friction and delays that come from juggling separate billing, payment, and accounting tools.
