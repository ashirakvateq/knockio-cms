What should work order management software include for contractors?
Work order management software for contractors should let you assign tasks and schedules to crews or subcontractors, track a detailed job budget against actual spend, generate material takeoffs linked to inventory, create purchase orders for shortages, and attach notes, photos, and vendor bills, all tied to the same job. Knockio brings every one of these into a single connected work order, which is more depth than most platforms offer without an expensive add on tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can work orders be assigned to subcontractors as well as internal crews?
Yes. Work orders support both internal crews and subcontractors or vendors, giving you flexibility no matter how your business is staffed.
What can a work order include?
A work order can include tasks, a detailed budget, materials and material takeoffs, notes, photos, a schedule, linked purchase orders, and vendor bills, everything needed to run the job from start to finish.
Can I create a purchase order directly from a work order?
Yes. If a material takeoff shows a shortage, you can generate a purchase order directly from the work order to restock inventory or order materials for the job.
Are work orders connected to invoicing and payments?
Yes. Work orders are part of Knockio's connected job workflow, so costs, materials, and progress tracked on a work order tie directly into your estimates, invoices, and payment tracking.
