Territory Management Software

Knockio's Territory Management Software helps teams draw, assign, and visualize sales territories on a map. Prevent two reps from visiting the same house and wasting payroll. See which areas are covered, in progress, or still open for new sales opportunities.

Schedule a Demo

How does territory management software prevent double knocking in door to door sales?
Territory management software prevents double knocking by giving each rep or team a clearly defined, map based area to work, then layering knocked door activity on top of those boundaries so managers can see exactly what has been covered. Knockio lets you draw unlimited custom territories, assign them to individual reps or teams, and view territory based reporting on leads and sales performance by area.
