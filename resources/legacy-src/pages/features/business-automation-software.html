Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

What kinds of tasks can I automate with Knockio?
You can automate payment reminders, task and schedule creation, email and text notifications, and more, all triggered by changes to job status, invoices, estimates, and inventory levels.
Do I need technical skills to build an automation?
No. Knockio's flow builder is built for home service business owners and office staff, not developers. Automations are built visually using your existing job statuses and triggers.
Can automations be customized to match my business's process?
Yes. Since job statuses in Knockio are fully customizable, your automations can be built around your exact workflow, whether you run a simple process or a multi stage pipeline.
Can automation help me get paid faster?
Yes. Automated payment reminders and text to pay follow ups are some of the most popular automations, helping businesses across every home service trade reduce overdue invoices without adding staff.
