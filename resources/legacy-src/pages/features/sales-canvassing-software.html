Most canvassing apps stop at a pin on a map. Knockio goes a lot further. Admins build territories and assign them to reps, track every rep live while they are in the field, and see exactly how far a rep actually was from a home when they dropped a pin. Reps drop a custom pin for every door, log a contact, book an appointment, run a presentation, create a task, and upload photos. Try our advanced sales canvassing software for your sales campaigns.

What is door to door canvassing software and what should it actually track?
Door to door canvassing software helps field sales teams organize territories, log activity at every home they visit, and report that activity back to the office in real time. Knockio handles this with custom territories assigned to specific reps, live GPS tracking of every rep in the field, and custom pin drops for every home, each with its own color, icon, and status. Reps can create contacts, book appointments, run presentations, log tasks, and upload media directly from the pin, while admins can see exactly how far a rep's device was from the property at the moment the pin was dropped, which is the part most canvassing apps leave out entirely.

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can I create custom territories for my canvassing team?
Yes. Admins can draw custom territories on a map and assign them to individual reps or teams, keeping the whole canvassing operation organized and avoiding overlap.
Can pins be customized with different statuses, colors, and icons?
Yes. You can create your own pin statuses, each with a unique color and icon, so the map gives an instant visual read on how a territory is being worked.
What can a rep do directly from a pin?
A rep can create a contact, set a task, book an appointment, run a presentation, and upload photos or other media, all directly from the pin without switching to a separate app.
How does Knockio provide location verification for field visits?
When a representative creates a property pin, Knockio records the distance between the device location and the selected property at that moment. Managers can use this information to review field records, identify possible location discrepancies, and maintain consistent territory data.
