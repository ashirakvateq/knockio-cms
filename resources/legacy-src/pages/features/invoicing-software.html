Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can I create an invoice without an estimate?
Yes. You can create invoices directly from a job or a customer record, not just from approved estimates. That gives you flexibility whether you run simple flat rate services or complex, multi phase projects.
Can customers pay an invoice online?
Yes. Every invoice can include a secure online payment link, so homeowners and commercial clients can pay by card, ACH, or other supported methods the moment they get it.
Will invoices automatically reflect job specific details?
Yes. Invoices pull job information, line items, and pricing straight from your estimates, work orders, or job records, so your team never has to type the same details twice.
Is Knockio just for invoicing, or does it cover the whole job?
Knockio is a full operations platform. Invoicing connects to your estimates, work orders, scheduling, inventory, and payments, all inside one system built specifically for home service businesses.
