Knockio’s GPS Tracking Software shows each rep’s real-time location, full movement history, and pin drop distance, so you can verify real field visits. Know who is working, where they went, and which visits actually happened.

Schedule a Demo

How does GPS tracking software verify if a field rep actually visited a property?
Knockio’s GPS tracking software calculates the distance between a rep’s device location and the property address at the exact moment they log an activity, such as dropping a pin for a knocked door. This pin drop distance check flags activity logged far from the actual property, helping managers confirm field reps are working real locations rather than reporting activity from a vehicle or somewhere else entirely.

Can I see where my reps are in real time?

Can I see where reps have been in the past?

How does Knockio verify if a pin drop is legitimate?
When a rep drops a pin for an activity like a knocked door, Knockio calculates the distance between the device’s location and the property address at that time, helping you identify pins dropped far from the actual location.
Is live tracking connected to jobs and leads?
Yes. Location and pin drop data are tied to the relevant job or lead record, giving you full context alongside activity history.
