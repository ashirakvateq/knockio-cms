Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Does Knockio support multiple warehouses or locations?
Yes. Knockio supports inventory by warehouse, branch, truck, or stock location, with location-specific quantities, transfers, allocations, low-stock thresholds, purchase orders, and reporting.
Can I track product variants and SKUs?
Yes. Knockio supports creating products and their variants with individual SKUs, along with cost and markup pricing for each one.
How do low stock alerts work?
Knockio notifies you when a product's stock level drops below your defined threshold, so you can reorder before it impacts a scheduled job.
Can material takeoffs from work orders update my inventory?
Yes. Material takeoffs created from work orders are linked to your inventory, helping you track what is allocated to jobs and what is available for new work.
Can purchase orders be used to receive inventory?
Yes. Purchase orders can be created on their own or from a material takeoff and used to receive new stock into your inventory.
