What should I look for in estimate software for contractors?
Good estimate software for contractors should let you build branded, itemized quotes fast, support digital signatures and online approval so customers can accept from any device, handle change orders when scope shifts mid job, and connect directly to your invoicing and inventory. Knockio includes all of this out of the box, plus optional line items for upsells and a reusable product and service catalog. Knockio connects estimate templates, optional line items, digital approvals, change orders, products, services, work orders, invoices, payments, and job history in one workflow.

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can customers approve and sign estimates online?
Yes. Knockio includes a customer acceptance portal with digital signature support, so homeowners and commercial clients can review and approve estimates from any device.
Does Knockio support change orders?
Yes. If the scope of work changes after an estimate is approved, you can create a change order linked to the original job and estimate, keeping pricing and approvals organized.
Can I offer optional add ons in an estimate?
Yes. Estimates support optional line items, letting customers choose upgrades or extras, which is a simple way to increase average job value for roofing, HVAC, solar, and remodeling jobs.
Can I save estimate templates for common jobs?
Yes. Knockio supports estimate templates and a product or service catalog, so your team can build accurate, consistent quotes quickly for repeatable job types.
