What is pipeline software for home service businesses and how does it work?
Pipeline software for home service businesses organizes leads, jobs, and work orders into visual boards with custom stages, so you can see exactly where everything stands at a glance. Knockio lets you build unlimited boards, create your own stages, sort jobs by how long they have sat in a stage or by total value, and assign specific boards to specific roles or departments, so a job can move between two boards at once if the same status exists on both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can I create more than one board?
Yes. You can create unlimited boards for leads, jobs, work orders, or any other workflow your business needs to track separately.
Can I sort jobs by how long they have been in a stage?
Yes. Boards let you sort by days in stage, so you can quickly spot leads or jobs that have gone stale and need attention.
Can I sort by job value?
Yes. You can sort by highest value, helping your team prioritize the jobs that matter most to revenue first.
Can I restrict who has access to a specific board?
Yes. Boards can be treated like a department. Assign specific roles to a board so only those roles can view it and move jobs through it.
What happens when a job's status exists on more than one board?
If a status exists on multiple boards, the job automatically appears in the matching stage on every board where that status exists, letting two departments work the same job without manual re-entry.
