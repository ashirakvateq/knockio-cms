Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

Can I control what different team members can see?
Yes. Knockio offers detailed, granular permissions so you can control exactly what each user or role can view and edit.
Can I create custom roles for my business?
Yes. You can create custom roles, such as Field Tech, Office Manager, or Subcontractor, with permissions tailored to each role’s responsibilities.
Can subcontractors have limited access to Knockio?
Yes. Subcontractors and vendors can be given limited access, such as visibility into only their assigned work orders, without exposing your full pipeline or financials.
Is it easy to onboard new team members?
Yes. New team members can be assigned an existing role, instantly giving them the appropriate access without manual configuration for each person.
Is Knockio useful for both sales and service teams?
Yes. Knockio is built for teams that need both sales and service workflows. Sales reps can manage leads and proposals, while office and field teams can manage scheduling, dispatching, job updates, invoices, and customer service history from the same CRM.
