Can I use my own business phone number for texting and calling customers?
Yes. Knockio lets you bring your own existing business phone number or get a new one directly inside the platform, then send and receive both calls and two way SMS texts from that number. You can also connect your existing business email, so every call, text, and email logs automatically to the right job record instead of disappearing into someone's personal phone.
