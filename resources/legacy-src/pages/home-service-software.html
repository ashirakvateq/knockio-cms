The questions every owner asks.

Will my crews actually use it?

That's the whole design constraint. Crews get a simple mobile app on the phone they already carry: clock in, take photos, update status. Three taps, no training manual. The complicated stuff — pipelines, costing, reports — lives on the office side.

How long does it take to switch?

You're up and running the same day. Import your customers, invite your team, and schedule your first job before the afternoon's out — not after a six-week implementation.

What does it cost?

$20–$60 per user per month, published right on the site. No custom-quote games, and no feature paywalls — every plan includes the full platform, including automations.

What does it replace?

Typically: a canvassing app, a CRM, a scheduling whiteboard or calendar tool, a timesheet app, an invoicing tool, and the group text. One subscription, one login, one job record from first contact to final payment.

Does the office see what happens in the field?

Instantly. When a crew clocks in, uploads photos, or marks a job complete, the office sees it in real time — no end-of-day phone calls, no "text me when you're done."
