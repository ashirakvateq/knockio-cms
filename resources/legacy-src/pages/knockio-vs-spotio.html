Knockio vs SPOTIO

SPOTIO is a strong field sales specialist: territories, rep tracking, and sales analytics, often layered on top of a CRM. It stops at the sale, so estimates, scheduling, and invoicing need a second system. Knockio covers the same canvassing ground and then runs the job all the way to the final payment in one platform.

Last reviewed July 2026 · Written by the Knockio team · Every figure is sourced below

The short answer

If you need to run the job after you close it, pick Knockio. SPOTIO fits teams that only sell, with fulfillment handled somewhere else.

Choose Knockio if

  • You want the knock and the job that follows it in one system
  • You would rather not buy a CRM and a service tool behind a canvassing app
  • You want published, flat pricing with no user minimums
  • You want the office to see field work in real time

Choose SPOTIO if

  • Your team only sells, and another system fulfills the work
  • You already run Salesforce or HubSpot and want a canvassing layer
  • You want deep territory analytics and rep leaderboards
  • You have five or more reps focused purely on selling

The honest read

What SPOTIO does well

  • Territory creation and mapping is a genuine core strength
  • GPS verified, one tap lead capture at the door
  • Leaderboards and activity tracking keep reps accountable
  • A mobile first app that field reps consistently like

Where it can fall short

  • Nothing after the sale: no scheduling, estimates, or invoicing
  • Offline sync problems are the most reported issue
  • Some reviewers question reporting accuracy
  • Quote only pricing with minimums makes cost hard to predict

Why teams pick Knockio instead

SPOTIO and Knockio overlap on the front half of the job. They part at the signature. SPOTIO hands the closed deal to another system. Knockio keeps it, turning a won deal into a scheduled job, field updates, and an invoice without changing apps.

That shapes the real cost too. A SPOTIO stack usually means SPOTIO plus a CRM plus an invoicing tool. Knockio covers the whole chain at a published $20 to $60 per user per month.

What to know before choosing Knockio

Knockio is a newer platform, so its integration marketplace and review history are smaller than long established tools. The upside of newer is speed: published pricing, same day setup, and a product that ships quickly. Pressure test everything on a live demo rather than taking our word for it.
