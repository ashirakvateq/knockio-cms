Knockio vs Jobber

Jobber is one of the most established field service platforms for small residential companies, and it earns its reputation for inbound, recurring work. Knockio covers the same quote to schedule to invoice workflow and adds what Jobber does not offer: door to door canvassing, sales pipelines, and territory mapping in one system.

Last reviewed July 2026 · Written by the Knockio team · Every figure is sourced below

The short answer

If you sell in the field, pick Knockio. You get canvassing, pipeline, scheduling, and invoicing on one flat published price. Jobber is the better pick only when your work is entirely inbound and recurring.

Choose Knockio if

  • You knock doors or sell in the field
  • You want pipeline, scheduling, and invoicing in one login
  • You want one flat price with every feature included
  • You want to be live the same day

Choose Jobber if

  • Your work is inbound and recurring, with no field sales
  • You want a long established tool with a big app marketplace
  • You rely on deep QuickBooks Online sync
  • Years of accumulated reviews matter more to you than breadth

The honest read

What Jobber does well

  • Mature and widely trusted, used by more than 250,000 home service pros
  • Published pricing you can evaluate without a sales call
  • A polished flow from quote to schedule to invoice to payment
  • Native QuickBooks Online sync and a large app marketplace

Where it can fall short

  • Reporting depth is the most common complaint in reviews
  • Per user fees grow quickly as the team scales
  • Some capabilities sit behind higher tiers or paid add ons
  • No canvassing or territory sales tooling at all

Why teams pick Knockio instead

The clearest difference is where the work starts. Jobber begins when a job is booked. Knockio begins at the door, then carries the same record through the sale, the schedule, the field, and the invoice. Nothing gets re typed between a sales app and a service tool.

Both publish pricing, which counts in both their favor. Jobber bills per plan tier plus per extra user. Knockio is a flat $20 to $60 per user per month with every feature included, which stays predictable as the crew grows.

What to know before choosing Knockio

Knockio is a newer platform, so its integration marketplace and review history are smaller than long established tools. The upside of newer is speed: published pricing, same day setup, and a product that ships quickly. Pressure test everything on a live demo rather than taking our word for it.

Common questions

Does Jobber have door knocking or canvassing?
No. Jobber is built for booked, inbound service work. Knockio includes canvassing, territory assignment, and live rep tracking in the same platform as job management.
Is Knockio cheaper than Jobber?
It depends on team size. Jobber runs $49 to $499 per month by tier with extra users at $29 each, as of July 2026. Knockio is a flat $20 to $60 per user per month with everything included. Compare on your real user count.
Does Jobber integrate with QuickBooks?
Yes, QuickBooks Online sync is one of its stronger points, though some users report fixing sync errors by hand.
Which is better for a roofing or exterior sales company?
If you sell at the door and manage production, Knockio fits that motion. Jobber suits recurring residential trades like lawn care and cleaning.
