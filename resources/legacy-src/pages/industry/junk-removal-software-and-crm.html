Junk Removal Software

Junk removal is a volume game. More bookings, more hauls, more trucks turning over jobs the same day, and a price that has to be quoted on the spot once the crew sees the pile. Knockio is junk removal software and CRM built for haulers who need one place to book jobs, quote by truckload, route crews, capture before-and-after photos, and collect payment the moment the truck is loaded.

What should junk removal companies look for in a CRM and booking platform?
A junk removal company should look for a CRM that moves as fast as the job does: quick online and phone booking, on-site volume-based quoting, crew and truck routing, before-and-after photo capture, and payment the second the load is gone. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It adds live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence and automated territory creation by zip code and city, so dispatch always knows which truck is closest to the next pickup. That speed, plus affordable pricing and support that actually answers, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out.
