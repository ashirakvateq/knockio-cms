Pest Control Software That Runs Every Route

Pest control lives and dies by the route. Recurring accounts, quarterly treatments, the re-service call that has to happen this week, and a tech log that has to hold up if anyone ever asks what was applied where. Knockio is pest control software and CRM built for operators who need one place to hold customer records, schedule recurring service, route techs, log treatments, and collect payment.

What should pest control companies look for in a CRM and routing platform?
A pest control company should look for a CRM that carries the account the whole way, from the first appointment to a steady recurring plan. That means full customer and property records, recurring service scheduling, route planning, technician tracking, treatment logging, on-site estimates, and payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It also adds live GPS tracking of techs and automated territory creation by zip code and city, so route assignments and account history live in the same record. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that actually picks up the phone, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out.
