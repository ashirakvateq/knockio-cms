Plumbing Software Built for Service and Repair

Plumbing runs on the call that cannot wait. A burst pipe at 7 a.m., a water heater swap by noon, a repipe that keeps a crew on site for two days. Knockio is plumbing software and CRM built for contractors who need one place to hold customer records, dispatch plumbers, quote jobs, track parts, and collect payment by milestone. No more juggling a paper schedule, a separate quoting tool, and a billing app that never lines up.

What should plumbing contractors look for in a CRM and dispatch platform?
A plumbing contractor should look for a CRM that carries the job from the booked call to the paid invoice. That means full customer and property records, scheduling and dispatch, plumber tracking, detailed estimates, work orders with parts, e-contracts, and milestone-based payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It adds live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence and a full job history per address, so the next visit starts with everything the last plumber already logged. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that picks up the phone, is what a generic CRM built for another trade leaves out.

Schedule and Dispatch

Quote and Build Work Orders

Detailed Estimates With Templates

Work Orders With Tasks, Budget, and Materials

Knockio carries a work order with tasks, a budget, and a material takeoff for every install, so pipe, fittings, valves, and fixtures are tracked against real numbers instead of a guess from the estimate.

Track Parts

Inventory Management for Plumbing Parts

Knockio tracks products, variants, SKUs, cost, and markup for pipe, fittings, valves, and fixtures, with low-stock alerts that flag a shortage before a plumber shows up to a job without the part.

Vendor Bills and Purchase Orders

Knockio raises a purchase order to your supply house when stock runs low and records the vendor bill against that purchase order or a work order, so suppliers spend ties straight to the job and your margins stay accurate.

Photo Annotation for Leaks and Repairs

Knockio lets plumbers snap a photo of a leak, a corroded line, or a failed water heater and mark it up with notes and arrows on the image, so the office can quote the fix and the customer sees exactly what was found.

Get Paid

Digital Contracts With E-Signatures

Knockio sends estimates and agreements for digital signature with a documented audit trail, IP and timestamp logged on every view. Change orders run the same way, so a mid-job addition is recorded and priced instead of argued later.

Milestone-Based Payment Collection

Knockio collects a deposit up front, progress draws as each phase of a larger job completes, and the final balance at the end, all tied to the job, so you get paid as the work moves instead of fronting parts and labor for days.

Flexible Payment Methods

Everything Plumbers Ask Before Switching to Knockio

Switching software is a real decision when your whole schedule, customer history, and job records live inside it. Here are the questions plumbing contractors ask most before making the move.

What should I look for in plumbing software?
Look for scheduling and dispatch, full job history per address, detailed estimating, work orders with parts, e-contracts, and flexible billing, all tied to one customer record. The platform should carry both emergency service and planned installs without forcing you into two tools. Knockio was built to run both from the same system.
Which software is best for a small plumbing business?
Knockio helps field service teams manage the full workflow. Teams can Manage Customers and Jobs, store customer details, create estimates, schedule jobs, assign crews, track job progress, upload photos, send invoices, and follow up with customers. Can Knockio bill a large job in stages?

Can Knockio track plumbing parts and materials?
Yes. Work orders carry a material takeoff, and inventory management tracks pipe, fittings, valves, and fixtures with low-stock alerts, so each job is cost against what it actually used.
Can Knockio track my plumbers in the field?
Yes. Knockio includes live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence, so you can confirm how close a plumber was to a property when they marked a job.
