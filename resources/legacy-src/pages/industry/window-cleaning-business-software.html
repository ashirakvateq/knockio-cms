Window Cleaning Business Software

Window cleaning runs on repeat customers and a route that has to stay tight. A first-time quote turns into a quarterly account, the crew works a neighborhood in one efficient loop, and the invoice goes out before they pull away from the curb. Knockio is window cleaning business software and CRM built for owners who need one place to hold customer records, schedule recurring cleans, route crews, send quotes, and collect payment.

What should window cleaning businesses look for in a CRM and scheduling platform?
A window cleaning business should look for a CRM that carries the customer the whole way, from the first quote to a steady recurring plan. That means full customer and property records, recurring scheduling, route planning, crew tracking, on-site quotes, and payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It adds live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence and automated territory creation by zip code and city, so route assignments and job history live in the same record. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that actually picks up, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out.

Yes. Crews can capture before-and-after photos at each stop and annotate them with notes right on the image, all saved to the customer record.
