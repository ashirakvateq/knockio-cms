Knockio handles all of it in one platform. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that picks up the phone, is what a generic CRM built for another trade leaves out.

Knockio tracks products, variants, SKUs, cost, and markup for wire, breakers, fixtures, and devices, with low-stock alerts that flag a shortage before an electrician shows up to a job without the part.

Yes. Knockio includes live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence, so you can confirm how close an electrician was to a property when they marked a job.
