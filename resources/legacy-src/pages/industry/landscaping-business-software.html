Landscaping Business Software

Landscaping covers a lot of ground, from recurring maintenance routes to full design-build projects with hardscaping, irrigation, and crews on site for weeks. Knockio is landscaping business software and CRM built to hold customer records, quote projects, schedule crews, track materials, and collect payment by milestone, all in one place instead of three apps that never agree.

What should landscaping companies look for in a CRM and project platform?
A landscaping company should look for a CRM that carries both sides of the business, the recurring maintenance route and the big design-build project. That means full customer and property records, recurring scheduling, detailed estimates, crew tracking, material and plant tracking, e-contracts, and milestone-based payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It adds live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence and automated territory creation by zip code and city, so routes, crews, and project history live in the same record. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that actually answers, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out.

Knockio helps landscaping contractors manage customer accounts, recurring routes, design-build projects, crews, materials, invoices, and payments. Land the Project

Detailed Estimates for Maintenance and Design-Build

Land the project during every visit, log outcomes, add notes or quick voice notes, and move prospects into a clear CRM pipeline, so your whole team sees the full history and no promising lead ever slips through the cracks between seasons.

One Record Per Customer and Property

Each account holds the address, contacts, custom fields, property size, gate codes, photos, and a full history of every visit, so the crew shows up knowing the scope, the layout, and the spots the homeowner flagged on the walk-through.

Communication History on Every Account

Every call, text, and email tied to a customer lands on the record, so when someone asks to adjust a plan or add a phase, the whole back-and-forth is right there instead of buried in a phone.

Schedule Maintenance

Recurring Routes and Project Scheduling

Run weekly maintenance routes and multi-week install projects from the same calendar. Recurring service builds itself out so the office is not rebooking by hand, while bigger projects move through their own stages.

Crew Assignment and Job Stage Tracking

Move each project through clear stages like estimate, approved, scheduled, in production, and final cleanup. Statuses are fully customizable, and unlimited boards let the office and the crews each see the view they care about.

Automated Territory Creation by Zip Code and City

Carve your maintenance area into routes automatically from zip codes and cities, instead of mapping every boundary by hand. As you grow into a new part of town, you can stand up a route in minutes. Most landscaping tools simply do not do this.

Track Materials and Costs

Work Orders With Budgets and Material Takeoffs

Track materials and costs from lead to service with notes, status updates, documents, customer details, photos, and clear next steps, so every landscaping project stays organized from first walkthrough to final invoice.

Inventory Management for Plants and Materials

Track products, variants, SKUs, cost, and markup for everything from sod to stone to sprinkler heads, with low-stock alerts so you reorder before a crew shows up to a job short on materials.

Vendor Bills and Material Purchase Orders

Raise a purchase order to your nursery or stone yard when stock runs low, then record the vendor bill against that purchase order or a work order, so what you spend at the supplier ties straight to the project and your margins stay honest.

Document and Media

Photo Annotation for Scope and Progress

Snap photos at the walk-through and through each phase, then mark them up with notes and arrows right on the image, so the crew knows exactly what goes where and the homeowner sees the progress before each payment.

Job Notes and Site Survey Photos

Drop detailed notes on every visit and use built-in surveys to capture site conditions, drainage issues, plant health, or extra work spotted, saved straight to the customer account instead of a slip lost in the truck.

Centralized File Storage Per Project

Keep design plans, permits, warranties, and signed agreements attached right to the project record, not scattered across email and a folder nobody can find when a question comes up next season.

Bill by Milestone

Milestone-Based Payment Collection

Collect a deposit up front, progress draws as each phase wraps, and a final balance at cleanup, all tied directly to the project. For a long design-build job, you get paid as the work moves instead of fronting materials and labor for weeks while you wait on one big check.

Digital Contracts With E-Signatures

Send the project agreement for digital signature with a documented audit trail, so the scope and price are locked in with a clean legal record before the first plant goes into the ground. Change orders work the same way, so an added phase is logged and priced instead of argued over later.

Flexible Milestone Payment Methods

Collect through tap to pay, text to pay, card, or ACH, all through Knockio's own built-in payment processing, so whether the customer pays on site or later from their phone, the money always lands tied to the right project.

Switching software is a real decision when your whole route, project history, and billing live inside it. Here are the questions landscaping contractors ask most before making the move.

What should I look for in landscaping software?
Look for a platform that handles both recurring maintenance and full design-build projects: recurring scheduling, detailed estimating, material tracking, e-contracts, and milestone billing, all tied to one customer record. Most tools do one side well and the other poorly. Knockio was built to run both from the same system.
Which software is best for a landscaping business that does both maintenance and installs?
Knockio helps field service teams manage the full workflow. Teams can Land the Project, store customer details, create estimates, schedule jobs, assign crews, track job progress, upload photos, send invoices, and follow up with customers. This helps reduce manual work and keeps everyone updated.
Can Knockio bill a project in stages?

Can Knockio track plants and materials per project?
Yes. Work orders carry a material takeoff, and inventory management tracks plants, hardscape, and irrigation parts with low-stock alerts, so each project is cost against what it actually used.
Can Knockio track my crews in the field?
Yes. Knockio includes live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence, so you can confirm how close a crew was to a property when they marked a job started or completed.
