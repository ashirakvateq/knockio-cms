What should HVAC contractors look for in a CRM and service platform?
An HVAC contractor should look for a CRM that carries the whole job, from the booked call to the paid invoice. That means customer and equipment records, scheduling and dispatch, technician tracking, estimates and service agreements, work orders with parts, e-signed approvals, and on-site payment. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It also adds live GPS tracking of techs and full job history per address, so the next visit starts with everything the last tech already learned. That depth of service record, plus affordable pricing and support that actually knows the trade, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out, and it is what makes an HVAC CRM for contractors worth keeping in the truck.

Everything HVAC Contractors Ask Before Switching to Knockio

Switching software is a real decision when your whole schedule, customer history, and equipment records live inside it. Here are the questions HVAC contractors ask most before making the move.

Can Knockio track equipment history per customer?
Yes. Each customer record holds the property's full service and equipment history, so a tech sees what is installed and what was done on past visits before arriving.
Can Knockio dispatch and track my techs in the field?
Knockio helps field service teams manage the full workflow. Teams can Manage Customers and Jobs, store customer details, create estimates, schedule jobs, assign crews, track job progress, upload photos, send invoices, and follow up with customers. This helps reduce manual work and keeps everyone updated.
Does Knockio handle work orders and parts for installs?
Yes. Work orders carry tasks, a budget, and a material takeoff, and you can raise a purchase order straight from the work order when a part runs short, with low-stock alerts to warn you first.
Does Knockio support digital contracts and on-site payment?
Yes. You can build estimates and service agreements, send them for e-signature, and collect payment through tap to pay, text to pay, card, or ACH, all tied to the job.
How is Knockio different from FieldPulse, FieldBoss, or ServiceTitan for HVAC companies?
ServiceTitan is heavy and priced for large shops, while FieldPulse and FieldBoss focus mostly on service management. Knockio brings dispatch, equipment history, estimates, work orders, and payments together, then adds live tech tracking, a fully customizable pipeline with unlimited boards, automated territory creation by zip and city, personalized support, and pricing that does not punish a growing company. It is one connected system built around the way HVAC service and install work actually runs.
