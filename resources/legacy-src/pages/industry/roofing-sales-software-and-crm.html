Manage Jobs

Customer and Property Records in One Place

Every job record holds the address, contacts, custom fields, photos, notes, tasks, and full ownership history. When a homeowner calls back about a repair, your team sees the whole history instead of digging through texts.

Dynamic Job Pipeline for Roofing Stages

Move jobs through clear stages like lead, inspection, estimate, proposal, approval, production, payment, and closeout. Statuses stay fully customizable, so you name and order them to match your own shop and run unlimited boards.

Crew and Roofing Production Scheduling

Assign crews to jobs, track production progress closely, and keep the office and the field reading one shared schedule instead of two versions of the truth. Everyone sees who is on which roof next.

Track the Field

Auto Territory Creation by ZIP & City

Build canvassing territories automatically from zip codes and cities, instead of drawing every single boundary by hand. After a storm, carve up a whole area and get reps knocking doors in minutes, not hours, unlike most roofing CRMs.

Live GPS Tracking of Every Roofing Rep

See where every canvasser and crew is in real time, which helps with accountability, safety, and knowing exactly who works which street right now. Pin drop verification confirms how close a rep stood when marking a door worked. See how door-to-door roofing reps use canvassing software.

Route Planning That Saves Daylight

Plan smarter, tighter routes so your roofing reps hit far more doors in less time each day, instead of crisscrossing the very same blocks over and over and burning the daylight hours that matter most after a storm.

Work Orders and Materials

Work Orders With Tasks, Budget, and Materials

Build a work order with tasks, a budget, and a material takeoff for every roof. The job is tracked against real numbers, not a rough guess from the estimate, and the office can see exactly what was used and what it cost.

Purchase Orders When Materials Run Short

When shingles or flashing are not in inventory, fire off a purchase order straight from the work order. Knockio tracks products, variants, SKUs, cost, and markup, and flags low stock before you run out mid-job.

Assign Jobs to Crews or Subcontractors

Work orders can go directly to your own in-house crew or to a trusted outside subcontractor, with tasks, notes, photos, and the full production schedule all attached, so whoever climbs the ladder on that roof always has the complete picture.

Documents and Media

E-Contracts With Legal Protection

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Roofers Ask Before Switching to Knockio

Switching software is a real decision when your whole pipeline, crew schedule, and customer history lives inside it. Here are the questions roofing contractors ask most before making the move.

Can Knockio keep all my job history in one record?
Yes. As a complete roofing contractor CRM, Knockio ensures every job record holds the property's contacts, photos, notes, documents, and full activity history, so anyone on your team sees the whole story instead of piecing it together from texts and email.
Can Knockio create canvassing territories automatically?
Yes. Our built-in roofing canvassing app can build territories automatically from zip codes and cities, so you can split up a storm-hit area and get reps knocking fast, without drawing every boundary by hand.
Can Knockio build estimates from EagleView measurement reports?
Yes. Knockio lets you create roof estimates using measurement data from EagleView or other measurement providers, so pricing is based on accurate roof data instead of a rough guess.
Does Knockio handle work orders and materials for production?
Yes. Functioning as a full roofing project management software, Knockio ensures work orders carry tasks, a budget, and a material takeoff. You can even raise a purchase order straight from the work order when materials run short, with low-stock alerts to warn you first.
How is Knockio different from Jobber or JobNimbus for roofing companies?
Knockio gives you the estimating, scheduling, and invoicing you would expect from tools like Jobber or JobNimbus, but as an all-in-one roofer software, it natively adds live rep tracking and automated territory creation for your sales team. It is one connected system built around the way a job actually runs, from the first knock to the final payment, backed by personalized support and pricing that does not punish a growing crew.
Is Knockio a roofing CRM or a door-to-door sales app?
Both, in one system. Knockio pairs door-to-door sales tools, like automatic canvassing territories, live GPS rep tracking, and route planning, with a full roofing CRM: job pipelines, crew scheduling, estimates, e-contracts, and payments. You do not need a separate canvassing app running alongside your CRM.
How is Knockio different from AccuLynx for roofing companies?
AccuLynx is built mainly for production and job management. Knockio adds native door-to-door sales tools on top of the CRM, including automated territory creation, live rep tracking on a map, and route planning, so sales and production run in one system instead of stitching a canvassing app onto your CRM.
