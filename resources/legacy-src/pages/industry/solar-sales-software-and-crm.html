What should solar companies look for in a CRM and sales platform?
A solar company should look for a CRM that carries the deal the whole way, from the first knock to a working system on the roof. That means complete customer records, door-to-door lead capture, a pipeline that matches solar stages, appointment setting between setters and closers, clear estimates, digital contracts, and deposit collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It also adds live GPS tracking of setters and automated territory creation by zip code and city, so a deal's full history lives in a single record. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that actually understands solar, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out.
