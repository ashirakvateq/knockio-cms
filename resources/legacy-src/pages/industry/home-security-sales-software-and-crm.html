What should home security sales teams look for in a canvassing and sales platform?
A home security sales team should look for a platform that matches the speed of a door-to-door push: automated territory assignment, live rep tracking, fast signup capture at the door, full conversation history per homeowner, e-signed agreements, and a pipeline that runs from first knock to confirmed install. Knockio covers all of it in one app. It adds automated territory creation by zip code and city plus live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence, so managers know exactly which doors were worked and by whom. That speed, plus affordable pricing and support that actually answers, is what generic CRMs built for office sales simply do not offer a field team.
