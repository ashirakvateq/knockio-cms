Restoration Job Management Software

Knockio is restoration job management software and CRM for contractors handling water, fire, and mold jobs. Manage customer records, damage photos, crews, equipment, materials, milestones, billing, and claim documentation in one place.

What should restoration contractors look for in a CRM and job platform?
A restoration contractor should look for a CRM that carries the job from first response through the insurance claim. That means full customer and property records, fast damage documentation with annotated photos, crew scheduling, work orders with equipment and materials, e-contracts, and milestone-based payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It adds live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence and a full job history per address, so every photo, note, and reading sits on one record the adjuster can be walked through. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that picks up the phone, is what a generic CRM built for another trade leaves out.

Why Restoration Pros Choose Knockio

Knockio helps restoration contractors manage customers, job documentation, scheduling, crews, equipment, materials, invoices, and payments. Centralized Records

One Record for Every Loss

Centralized Records during every visit, log outcomes, add notes or voice notes, and move prospects into a clear CRM pipeline.

Customizable Job Pipeline

Knockio moves each job through stages you define, such as new loss, inspection, mitigation, drying, rebuild, and claim, so owners catch a job stuck waiting on an adjuster before it stalls.

Communication History Per Job

Knockio saves every call, text, and email with the homeowner, adjuster, and crew on the job record, so the full thread of a claim sits in one place instead of scattered across inboxes.

Respond & Schedule

Crew Scheduling and Dispatch

Knockio assigns first-response and rebuild crews to the right job and keeps the office and the field on the same calendar, so an emergency loss gets a crew moving without scrambling the whole day.

GPS Tracking With Proximity

Knockio shows where every crew is in real time and uses proximity intelligence to confirm how close they were to the address when they marked a job worked, so dispatch sends the closest crew to a new loss and the activity log holds up.

Automated Territory Creation

Knockio builds service territories automatically from zip codes and cities, so after a storm event you can carve up the affected area and balance crews by region in minutes instead of mapping boundaries by hand.

Document Every Loss

Photo Annotation for Records

Document every loss from lead to service with notes, status updates, documents, customer details, photos, and clear next steps, so every restoration job stays fully organized from first contact through final billing.

Job Notes, Surveys, and Readings

Knockio captures detailed notes and survey answers on every visit, so moisture readings, drying logs, and site conditions are recorded straight to the job instead of a paper slip lost on site.

Centralized File Storage Per Job

Knockio keeps the estimate, photos, drying logs, and signed agreements attached to the job record, so when the adjuster asks for documentation it is all in one place, not pulled from five sources.

Manage Equipment & Materials

Work Orders With Tasks, Budget, and Materials

Knockio carries a work order with tasks, a budget, and a material takeoff for the rebuild, so drywall, flooring, and finish materials are tracked against real numbers instead of a guess from the estimate.

Inventory Management for Equipment and Supplies

Knockio tracks products, variants, SKUs, cost, and markup for air movers, dehumidifiers, and rebuild supplies, with low-stock alerts so you know what is available before assigning it to a job.

Vendor Bills and Purchase Orders with Knockio

Knockio raises a purchase order to your supplier when stock runs low and records the vendor bill against that purchase order or a work order, so subcontractor and supplier spend ties straight to the job and your claim numbers stay accurate.

Everything Restoration Pros Ask Before Switching to Knockio

Switching software is a real decision when your whole job documentation, claim history, and billing live inside it. Here are the questions restoration contractors ask most before making the move.

What should I look for in restoration software?
Look for fast damage documentation, full job history per address, work orders with equipment and materials, e-contracts for authorizations, and flexible billing, all tied to one job record. The platform should hold everything an adjuster might ask for in one place. Knockio was built to run mitigation and rebuild from the same system.
Which software is best for a small restoration company?
Knockio helps field service teams manage the full workflow. Teams can Centralized Records, store customer details, create estimates, schedule jobs, assign crews, track job progress, upload photos, send invoices, and follow up with customers. This helps reduce manual work and keeps everyone updated.
Can Knockio document damage for insurance claims?
Yes. Knockio captures annotated photos, notes, surveys, and readings on the job record, and stores the estimate, drying logs, and signed authorizations together, so the documentation an adjuster needs is in one place.
Can Knockio bill a job in stages?
Yes. Knockio supports milestone-based payment collection, so you can take a deposit, bill progress draws as mitigation and rebuild phases complete, and collect the final balance at closeout, all tied to the job.
Can Knockio track my crews and equipment?
Yes. Knockio includes live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence for crews, and inventory management tracks air movers, dehumidifiers, and supplies, so you know who and what is assigned to each job.
