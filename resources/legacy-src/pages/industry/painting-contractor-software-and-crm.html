Painting Contractor Software

Painting is a walk-through business. You measure the rooms, count the doors and trim, price the prep and the coats, and then a crew has to show up, knock out the work, and get paid before the next estimate is even booked. Knockio is painting contractor software and CRM built for painters who need one place to hold customer records, quote jobs, schedule crews, track materials, and collect payment.

What should painting contractors look for in a CRM and estimating platform?
A painting contractor should look for a CRM that carries the job from the first walk-through to the final coat. That means full customer and property records, detailed estimates for prep, primer, and coats, crew scheduling, material and paint tracking, e-contracts, and payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform. It adds live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence and automated territory creation by zip code and city, so estimates, crews, and job history live in the same record. That depth, plus affordable pricing and support that actually answers, is what a generic CRM built for another industry leaves out.

What should I look for in painting contractor software?
Look for detailed estimating that breaks out prep and coats, crew scheduling, material tracking, e-contracts, and payment collection, all tied to one customer record. Which software is best for a small painting business?
Knockio helps field service teams manage the full workflow. Teams can Win the Job, store customer details, create estimates, schedule jobs, assign crews, track job progress, upload photos, send invoices, and follow up with customers. This helps reduce manual work and keeps everyone updated.
Can Knockio track paint and materials per job?
Yes. Work orders carry a material takeoff, and inventory management tracks paint, primer, and supplies with low-stock alerts, so each job is cost against what it actually used.
Can Knockio handle change orders mid-job?
Yes. You can send change orders for digital signature the same way as the original agreement, so an added room or an upgraded finish is documented and priced instead of handled loosely.
Does Knockio track my crews in the field?
Yes. Knockio includes live GPS tracking with proximity intelligence, so you can confirm how close a crew was to a property when they marked a job started or completed.
