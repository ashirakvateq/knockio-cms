Knockio vs JobNimbus

JobNimbus is a roofing first CRM known for visual production boards and roofing integrations such as aerial measurement. It has no door to door canvassing and does not publish pricing. Knockio covers the same pipeline to invoice chain, adds canvassing and territory mapping, and shows its price right on the site.

Last reviewed July 2026 · Written by the Knockio team · Every figure is sourced below

The short answer

Roofers who knock doors will get more from Knockio: sales and production in one system with a published price. Pick JobNimbus if aerial measurement and supplier integrations outweigh canvassing for your business.

Choose Knockio if

  • Your leads come from knocking as well as referrals
  • You want a price you can read before any sales call
  • You want sales and production in one system
  • You want a live map of reps and jobs together

Choose JobNimbus if

  • You lean on aerial measurement tools like EagleView and HOVER
  • You want board driven workflows tuned to roofing stages
  • You track insurance claims and adjusters in your pipeline
  • You want a roofing focused tool with a long trade record

The honest read

What JobNimbus does well

  • Drag and drop production boards that roofers find intuitive
  • Strong roofing integrations: aerial measurement, suppliers, QuickBooks
  • Insurance claim and adjuster tracking for storm restoration
  • A capable automation engine for follow ups and stage triggers

Where it can fall short

  • No published pricing, and the layered model is hard to estimate
  • The built in email tool draws recurring complaints
  • Reviews flag mobile app stability issues
  • Reporting depth is thin for some users

Why teams pick Knockio instead

Both platforms give roofers a pipeline, estimates, scheduling, and invoicing. The gap is the front of the funnel. JobNimbus expects the lead to exist. Knockio can create it at the door and move it through the same boards into production.

On transparency, Knockio publishes a flat $20 to $60 per user per month with everything included. JobNimbus is quote only. If deep roofing integrations matter more to you than canvassing and a public price, JobNimbus is still worth a look.

What to know before choosing Knockio

Knockio is a newer platform, so its integration marketplace and review history are smaller than long established tools. The upside of newer is speed: published pricing, same day setup, and a product that ships quickly. Pressure test everything on a live demo rather than taking our word for it.

Common questions

Does JobNimbus have door knocking or canvassing?
No. Leads live in CRM boards, not on a knocking map. Knockio includes canvassing, territories, and live rep tracking with the same CRM features.
How much does JobNimbus cost?
It does not publish prices; the pricing page is a request form, as of July 2026. Knockio publishes $20 to $60 per user per month.
Which is better for roofing?
Both serve roofers. Knockio adds canvassing and a published price. JobNimbus has deeper roofing specific integrations. Choose by which matters more to how you sell.
Does JobNimbus integrate with QuickBooks and aerial measurement?
Yes. QuickBooks, EagleView, HOVER, and supplier ordering are among its stronger integrations.
