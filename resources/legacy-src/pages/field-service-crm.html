Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Service CRM

Service teams need more than a basic contact list. Knockio connects customer records, leads, jobs, estimates, schedules, crews, invoices, payments, and field updates. It helps sales, office, and field teams work from the same information, so jobs move faster with less confusion.

What is Knockio Service CRM?
Knockio Service CRM is a customer and field service management system that covers everything from the first lead to the final payment: customer details, jobs, crews, schedules, estimates, and invoices. It keeps that data connected so sales teams, office staff, and field crews all work from the same system.
How does Knockio help field service teams?
Knockio covers the full workflow: capturing leads, storing customer details, creating estimates, scheduling jobs, assigning crews, tracking job progress, uploading photos, sending invoices, and following up with customers afterward.
Can Knockio manage customer and job history?
Yes. Knockio keeps a customer's notes, past visits, job details, photos, documents, invoices, payments, and messages together on their profile, so your team can see what happened before and what's next.
Does Knockio support estimates and invoices?
Yes. Create estimates and digital proposals, then move approved work straight into scheduling, invoicing, and payment — all inside the same CRM.
Is Knockio useful for both sales and service teams?
Yes — Knockio connects sales activity with service delivery, so leads captured in Knockio's canvassing app can move into scheduled jobs without switching tools.
