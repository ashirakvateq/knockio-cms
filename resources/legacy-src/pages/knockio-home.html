The Problem

Field Teams Are Stuck Juggling Too Many Tools

Most field teams do not lose work because of team inefficiency. They lose work because they do not have a smart process.

No Shared Job Record

Managers cannot clearly see where reps are, what they finished, which areas were covered, or which leads need action.

Weak Field Visibility

Lead notes, customer details, photos, tasks, and updates often sit in different places, so teams repeat work and miss context.

Missed Follow-Ups

Hot leads go cold when calls, reminders, proposals, signatures, and next steps are not tied to one clear workflow.

Slow Billing

Invoices and payments take longer when job details, approvals, pricing, and customer records are not connected.

Choosing the right field software depends on your team’s workflow, budget, industry, and growth plans. Some tools are stronger for door-to-door sales. Some are built more for service jobs. Knockio brings both together with canvassing, CRM, scheduling, routing, dispatching, proposals, invoices, and payments.

Knockio is a field sales and field service platform. It handles door-to-door canvassing, territory mapping, route planning, and lead capture on the sales side, and job scheduling, dispatching, estimates, and invoicing on the service side. Most teams juggle a separate tool for each; Knockio keeps them in one workflow saving both additional costs and time.
Knockio is best for both field sales and service teams in roofing, solar, HVAC, pest control, fiber, and other home-services industries. Knockio is for anyone managing reps or crews in the field, from the first door knock to the final invoice.
