Everything Teams Ask Before Using Knockio Canvassing

Canvassing teams need more than maps. Knockio helps reps capture leads, manage routes, update visits, and follow up faster. It also connects CRM, estimates, proposals, scheduling, invoices, and field service workflows, so teams can manage the full process with less confusion.

What is Knockio canvassing software?
Knockio canvassing software helps field teams manage door-to-door sales and field visits. Reps can capture leads, view assigned areas, update visit results, add notes, upload photos, and set follow-ups from the field. Managers can track activity, manage territories, and move leads into the next sales or service step.
Is Knockio only for door-to-door sales teams?
No. Knockio's canvassing software works for door-to-door sales teams, roofing crews, solar dealers, pest control companies, fiber sales teams, HVAC contractors, and any business that manages field reps or territory-based outreach. It even has a plan for teams needing canvassing plus CRM and service workflow tools.
How does Knockio help managers track canvassing teams?
Managers can see field activity, assigned territories, lead status, and rep progress in one place. This makes it easier to know who is working where, which leads need attention, and which areas still need coverage. It also reduces the need for constant check-in calls.
Can Knockio turn canvassing leads into jobs?
Yes. Every lead a rep captures is saved with full visit history and contact details, ready to hand off the moment it's qualified. Teams that also use Knockio's field service CRM can move that same lead straight into scheduling and job management — see how that works on our Field Service CRM page.
Does Knockio work on mobile?
Yes. Knockio is made for field teams, so mobile access is an important part of the workflow. We have both an Android app & an iOS app so while working outside, updating leads, adding notes, checking customer details, and managing their daily field activity is not a hassle.
